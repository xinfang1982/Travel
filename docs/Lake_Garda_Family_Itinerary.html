Keep the week easy

  • Breakfast is normally 07:30–10:00 and dinner 18:30–20:30—double-check at reception.
  • Ask for the latest free shuttle timetable when you check in.
  • Book Gardaland and Sigurtà tickets ahead.
  • Arrive at the ferry ticket office at least 20–30 minutes early.
  • Pack hats, sunscreen, refillable bottles and water shoes.
  • Keep outdoor walks for mornings and pool time for hot afternoons.
