A relaxed five-day plan

One main adventure each day, with room for weather and tired feet.

Sunday · 16 August
DriveDOM

1 · Arrive and settle in

Allow 6–7 hours plus breaks, check in, then take a gentle Binnenalster stroll. If everyone still has energy, the Summer DOM is open 14:00–23:00; it is entirely optional after the drive.

Dinner: keep it easy near the hotel.
Monday · 17 August
TicketsRoute

2 · Miniatur Wunderland & HafenCity

Take an early timed slot and allow around three hours. Lunch in Speicherstadt, explore the canals, then finish with a pre-booked Elbphilharmonie Plaza view. No need to add another museum.

Book early: its 25th anniversary is during this trip.
Tuesday · 18 August
Ferry mapRoute

3 · Harbour, tunnel & ferry 62

Start at Landungsbrücken, walk through the Old Elbe Tunnel, then ride ferry 62. Stop at Museumshafen Oevelgönne or the Elbe beach in good weather before returning by ferry.

The harbour ferry is included with a valid HVV ticket.
Wednesday · 19 August
Zoo infoRoute

4 · Tierpark Hagenbeck

Arrive near opening and spend an unhurried day with animal areas, playground and meal breaks. If heavy rain is forecast, swap days and use the International Maritime Museum as the indoor fallback.

Main full-day outing—keep the evening free.
Thursday · 20 August
Drive home

5 · Old town, lunch, home

After checkout, make a short loop around Rathaus, Alsterarkaden and Jungfernstieg. Have an early lunch and leave before afternoon traffic for the return to Plankstadt.

Keep this intentionally light: it is a long driving day.

Before you go

  • Choose a hotel with parking and U-/S-Bahn access.
  • Reserve Miniatur Wunderland and Plaza time slots.
  • Check Hagenbeck hours and weather.
  • Compare HVV day and group tickets.
  • Download the HVV app and offline maps.
  • Check live traffic before both drives.
