A relaxed week

Assumption: you arrive and depart via Valencia and stay in or near the city centre. Keep Christmas Eve and Christmas Day flexible.

Weather: no reliable daily forecast is available this far ahead. Checked 9 Nov 2026; forecast unavailable for these dates. Time zone: Europe/Madrid.

Wednesday · 23 December

Arrive and settle in

Check in, unpack, and take a gentle walk around Plaça de l’Ajuntament to see the city-centre decorations. Skip any timed attraction on arrival day.

Meal note: keep dinner close to your accommodation; reserve if arriving late. Weather: Forecast not yet available for this date.

Thursday · 24 December

Old Town and Central Market

Explore the Central Market and nearby old-town sights in the morning. Its usual schedule is Monday–Saturday, 7:30–15:00, but Christmas Eve hours may differ. Keep the afternoon open and unhurried.

Meal note: have lunch early and book dinner ahead; some places may close early. Weather: Forecast not yet available for this date.

Friday · 25 December

Christmas Day outdoors

Keep this as a low-key day: take a walk through the Turia Gardens and, if conditions suit, stop at Gulliver Park. Treat indoor attractions as closed unless their dated calendars confirm otherwise.

Meal note: reserve a Christmas meal in advance; do not rely on walk-in availability. Weather: Forecast not yet available for this date.

Saturday · 26 December

Oceanogràfic and City of Arts and Sciences

Make the Oceanogràfic the main outing and allow several hours. Add the Science Museum only if you have energy; both are in the same complex. Select the visit date in the official calendar before buying tickets.

Meal note: plan lunch near the complex or check on-site dining hours. Weather: Forecast not yet available for this date.

Sunday · 27 December

Old Town at a slower pace

Wander around Plaça de la Mare de Déu and the Cathedral quarter, then enjoy a flexible lunch and a second stroll through the historic centre. Check opening hours before planning an indoor visit.

Meal note: reserve Sunday lunch if you have a particular restaurant in mind. Weather: Forecast not yet available for this date.

Monday · 28 December

Optional Albufera and El Palmar

If bus and boat services are running, take a half-day trip to Albufera and have paella in El Palmar. Confirm the winter timetable and book a boat only after checking availability. If weather or schedules do not cooperate, keep this as a free city day.

Meal note: make lunch the main meal on the excursion; check restaurant opening days. Weather: Forecast not yet available for this date.

Departure day

Have breakfast, check out, and head to the airport or station according to your booking. Keep the morning free rather than squeezing in another attraction.
